How are live plants safely packaged for delivery in Chennai? +

All our live plants are packed in specialized ventilated, sturdy corrugated boxes with custom protective frames and moisture-retaining coco-fiber covers over the root ball. This ensures zero soil spillage and keeps the foliage upright and fresh during transit.

Do you offer doorstep delivery across all Chennai areas? +

Yes! We deliver across all Chennai pin codes (including Medavakkam, Velachery, OMR, Tambaram, Anna Nagar, Adyar, and Porur). Orders above ₹999 qualify for Free Home Delivery.

What is your 100% plant replacement guarantee? +

We provide a 100% Healthy Plant Guarantee. In the rare event that a plant arrives damaged or unhealthy, simply send us a photo on WhatsApp (+91 99625 45455) within 48 hours of delivery, and we will dispatch a free replacement or provide store credit immediately.

How often should I water my indoor & outdoor plants in Chennai? +

For outdoor plants, water daily in the early morning or evening during Chennai summers. For indoor plants (like Snake Plant, Money Plant, and Peace Lily), check that the top 1-2 inches of soil are dry before watering (usually once every 5 to 7 days). Overwatering is the most common mistake!
